Apartment Rent Change by Seattle Census Tract

This interactive visualization shows apartment rent trends across Seattle census tracts. Users can compare median rent, year-over-year rent change, and percent rent change to understand where rent pressure may be increasing. This project uses D3.js for data loading, scales, axes, transitions, and event listeners. I referenced D3 documentation for transitions, events, and CSV loading, and used the City of Seattle Open Data Portal dataset “Apartment Market Rent Prices by Census Tract.”

Data Source: City of Seattle Open Data Portal — Apartment Market Rent Prices by Census Tract.
